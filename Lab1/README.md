
# Airport Lost Luggage Claim & Tracking Portal

## Software Engineering Lab 1

**Course:** Requirements Engineering & UML Use-Case Modelling

## Problem Statement

Develop an airline baggage recovery platform that enables passengers to report lost baggage, track recovery status, and receive compensation updates. The system maintains baggage scan timelines across airport transit hubs and assists baggage service agents in locating and returning lost luggage.
